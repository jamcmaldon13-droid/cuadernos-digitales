01 · Gramática y matices de registro

En C1/C2, la dificultad no es únicamente escoger la preposición, sino controlar el grado de certeza, la cortesía, la estructura informativa y las implicaturas.

06 · Comprensión crítica e inferencia

The Difference Between Agreement and Assent

At the quarterly review, a department head circulated a note stating that the proposed restructuring had received “broad support.” Several colleagues interpreted the phrase as evidence of unanimous approval. Maya, who had attended the consultation, considered the wording misleading: while no one had opposed the underlying objective, a number of participants had expressed reservations about the timetable. Rather than accuse the author of deliberately misrepresenting the discussion, she asked whether the note could distinguish support for the principle from agreement on its implementation. The department head initially replied that the distinction was merely semantic. Maya explained that the ambiguity mattered because the document would inform decisions by colleagues who had not been present. She suggested replacing “broad support” with “general support for the objective, subject to concerns about timing and resources.” The revision was adopted. The exchange illustrated how a carefully framed question can correct an impression without turning a disagreement into a personal confrontation.
