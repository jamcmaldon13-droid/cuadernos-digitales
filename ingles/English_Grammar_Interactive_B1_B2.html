1. Guía de estructuras

InglésEjemploEspañol
ask + personAsk her a question.Hazle una pregunta.
ask + person + to + verbAsk him to wait.Pídele que espere.
ask + person + for + thingAsk me for help.Pídeme ayuda.
tell + person + informationTell her the truth.Dile la verdad.
say + something + to + personSay hello to him.Salúdalo.
speak/talk + to + personSpeak to me.Habla conmigo.
explain + thing + to + personExplain it to us.Explícanoslo.

No digas ask to her, tell to me ni explain me the problem. La estructura depende del verbo.

6. Comprensión lectora · 6 puntos

A Misunderstanding at Work

On Monday morning, Emma received a message from her colleague Daniel: “Please send the report today.” She assumed he needed the final version before lunch, so she rushed to finish it. However, Daniel had only asked her to send a draft by the end of the day.

When they met, Emma told him that she had skipped her break to complete the report. Daniel was surprised. “I should have explained the deadline more clearly,” he said to her. Emma replied that she could have asked him for clarification instead of making an assumption.

They decided to speak to their manager about improving communication within the team. Their manager listened carefully and asked them to suggest a practical solution. Emma proposed including exact times in important messages, while Daniel suggested confirming expectations before starting urgent tasks.

In the end, the misunderstanding became a useful lesson: asking questions early can save time and prevent unnecessary stress.
